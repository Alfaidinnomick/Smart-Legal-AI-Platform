The current design system operates in a **light color mode**, ensuring a modern and comfortable visual experience, especially in low-light environments.

**Color Palette:**
The system uses a distinct color palette to define its visual identity:
- **Primary Color:** `#0F172A` (A very dark, almost black, blue tone, often used for dominant elements.)
- **Secondary Color:** `#2563EB` (A vibrant blue, suitable for interactive components and highlights.)
- **Tertiary Color:** `#10B981` (A bright green, providing an additional accent or status indicator.)
- **Neutral Color:** `#F8FAFC` (A very light, almost white, off-white for backgrounds and neutral surfaces, used effectively within the light mode context for contrast.)

**Typography:**
- **Headline Font:** `manrope` - Provides a clean, modern feel for headings.
- **Body Font:** `inter` - Chosen for its legibility and versatility in body text.
- **Label Font:** `inter` - Consistent with body text for clear and readable labels.

**Shapes and Spacing:**
- **Roundedness:** `2` - Elements feature a moderate level of roundedness, striking a balance between soft and sharp aesthetics.
- **Spacing:** `2` - The system employs a normal level of spacing between UI elements, ensuring good readability and visual comfort without being too dense or sparse.
